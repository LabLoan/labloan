# Booking rules

Last reviewed: 2025-03-04

## Slots

- A booking has a start and an end time.
- The maximum length of a single booking is **8 hours**.
- Bookings for the same equipment must not overlap.

## Limits per student

- A student can have at most **3 active bookings** at a time. A booking is active
  until it ends or is cancelled.

## Cancellations

- A student can cancel any booking before it starts.
- A cancellation less than **24 hours** before the start counts as late and costs
  **2 penalty points**.

## Returns

- Equipment returned late costs **1 penalty point** per day.

## Equipment in repair

- Equipment that is marked as in repair cannot be booked.
