# Approval flow

Last reviewed: 2025-02-11

## Which bookings need approval

Bookings for restricted equipment (3D printers, the laser cutter) wait for approval.
Bookings for all other equipment are approved at once.

## Who approves

The **Lab Manager** approves or rejects bookings for restricted equipment. The Lab
Manager sees pending bookings in the order they were made.

## Outcome

- When a booking is approved, the student gets a confirmation email.
- When a booking is rejected, the student gets an email with the reason.
